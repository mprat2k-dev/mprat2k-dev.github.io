Working frome home is overrated. Counterproductive, even, if you want to do something frustrating and difficult. On a day-to-day basis. The space itself becomes poisoned.  Even thoguh your laptop is shut and the clock is punched, there's an oppressive phantasm that lingers in the air. You're still agitated and you can't calmp down. You need to go somewhere else.

Diners, I think, are a good middle ground between a fast-food joint and a cafe. They're open late, usually uncrowded, and therefore the staff aren't in any rush to shoo you out. And in going into a public place, you place limitations on yourself. Sure, you have your coffee and pancakes to reward yourself for a job well done, but you can't really *do* much except what you've prepared to do. And, while there's no rush, it's not comfortable to sit around all day. Your butt is going to get sore and start sticking to the vinyl booths, and surely you don't want to leave your bag unattended while you go on a walk?

Sure, a Grand Slam isn't the most economical way to get protein and caffiene in your body, but you're paying to get some extra productivity out of what would have been a dull, rainy evening. Such is life.
